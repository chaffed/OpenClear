A guided checklist for implementing OpenClear for your organization — each step links to the real page where you actually do it. Steps with a green check are detected automatically from your data (e.g. "Add your accounts" turns green the moment any account exists); steps with a checkbox have no single "done" fact to detect and you mark them yourself. Optional steps (like single sign-on) don't need to be completed for the checklist to be considered done — plenty of organizations won't use them.

See RUNBOOK.md in the project repository for the same checklist as a standalone document, including a prerequisites list to gather beforehand.
