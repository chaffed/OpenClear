An authorization is a standing rule pre-approving future debits/credits from one originator against an account — the whitelist ACH transactions are matched against.

  • Receiver scope — "any (blanket)" covers every receiver from that originator; otherwise it's limited to the one receiver ID shown.
  • Max amount / Freq/30d / SEC codes — "no limit"/"any" means that dimension isn't restricted.
  • Revoking is one-way — there's no "un-revoke."
  • To review every ACH debit on an account, the way reverse positive pay works for checks, give it no authorizations: each debit then comes to you to decide.
