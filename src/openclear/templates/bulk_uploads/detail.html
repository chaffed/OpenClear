The original file is kept exactly as submitted, signed with a server-held key — "signature verified" re-checks it against a fresh read of the file right now, not a cached flag, so any tampering after upload shows up immediately.

Backing an upload out reverses what it created: real records (issued items, memberships) are voided/deactivated; accounts and ACH transactions (which have no such concept) are only marked as originating from a reversed upload. It's one-way, and only available for uploads made after this feature shipped — an older upload has nothing tracked to reverse.
