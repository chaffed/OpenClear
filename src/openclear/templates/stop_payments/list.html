A stop payment blocks a specific check from clearing — a presented check matching an active stop (by check number, account, and amount if one was given) is flagged as an exception instead of matching normally, even if it would otherwise match an issued item exactly.

  • Leaving Amount blank matches any amount presented for that check number.
  • Leaving Expires blank means the stop never expires on its own.
  • Cancelling is one-way — there's no "un-cancel."
