Fraud scoring estimates how likely each exception is to be paid or returned. Your organization chooses which model does that scoring.

  • The shared model learns from the decisions of every participating bank, so it's useful from day one. The platform operator trains it and decides which version is active.
  • A bank-only model learns only from your own decisions, so your data never leaves your organization. Your administrators retrain it on the Admin page.

A customer can still have its own model on top of either (Customers → ML scoring).

  • Your administrators retrain your model on the Admin page. A retrained version replaces the active one only once it has learned from at least {{ min_bank_decisions }} of your decisions and scores at least as well on your recent ones.
