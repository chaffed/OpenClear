When you need help from the people who run OpenClear, grant one of them access here, for a stated reason and a set time. They work under their own name, with a banner saying it's your grant, and the access ends by itself when the time is up, or as soon as you end it.

Read-only access lets them see what your staff see, except secrets, users, security groups, settings and the action log. Write access also lets them enter and correct items, never decide them; it needs a second admin's approval before it starts.

Every grant, approval, use and end is in your action log.
