Data Dictionary & Schema

Every table and column in the database, generated by reading the application's own SQLAlchemy models directly — not a stale copy that can drift from the real schema. Grouped into six subsystems, each with an ER diagram (entities and their key/foreign-key columns only) followed by a full column-level data dictionary for every table in that group. Diagrams are draggable and support scroll-to-zoom; use the corner buttons for click-to-zoom or to reset the view. The PDF export (Download PDF on the Bank Administrator Documentation index) gives each diagram its own full landscape page for legibility, and appends a row-count summary for every table in your organization at the very end.

1. Tenancy & Access

The multi-tenant backbone: a tenant is one bank; a customer is that bank's own business client; a tenant_membership row is what actually grants a user access, scoped either bank-wide or to one customer, governed by a security_group's permission set.

scim_user, scim_group, scim_group_member

People and groups the bank's directory provisioned over SCIM, per connection. scim_user keeps the directory's own names (userName, externalId, display name, given and family name, active) and links to the OpenClear user (matched by email). scim_group has the group's displayName (matched against sso_group_mapping.external_group) and externalId; scim_group_member links them. 4. Exceptions, Decisions & ML

A check or ACH item that didn't match cleanly becomes an exception_item; resolving it produces exactly one decision. ml_model holds trained fraud-scoring model versions: the shared model, a bank's own bank-only model, or a customer's model. payment_network is the small, fixed lookup table (check, ach) almost every other table's network_code refers to.
