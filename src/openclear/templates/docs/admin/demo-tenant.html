Demo Tenant

If your deployment has a demo tenant enabled, it's a fully functioning, pre-populated organization sales teams can show off to prospects — real issued items, exceptions, users, and its own trained ML model, without touching any real customer's data.

How resets work

  • Hourly reset — the demo resets on a fixed schedule (every hour by default), so whatever one visitor changed doesn't stay in place for the next. Anyone signed in at that moment is sent back to the sign-in page. A reset also restores the organization's own settings, such as banner and login messages, colors, and password rules.
  • Automatic reset — if the demo tenant sits idle past its configured session window, the very next login to it wipes any changes a previous demo made and reseeds it fresh, before that login is even processed. Prospects always see a clean demo.
  • Manual reset — from Admin, an administrator can trigger a reset on demand, useful right before a scheduled demo rather than waiting for the idle window to lapse.

This action is scoped tightly to the actual flagged demo tenant — it can't be triggered against a real customer's organization.

What's locked in the demo

Everyone signs into the demo with the same shared credentials, so a few actions that would let one visitor lock out or disrupt the others are turned off. Trying one shows a message explaining why:

  • Single sign-on settings and security groups
  • Editing, deactivating, unlocking, resetting, or signing out existing users, and granting access to people from other organizations
  • The shared account's password, security keys, and "sign out other devices"
  • Branding (name and logo) and session timeouts
  • Data exports, and ML model retraining or activation

Everything else works normally: issued items, paid items, exceptions and decisions, ACH, customers, messages, and adding new users.
