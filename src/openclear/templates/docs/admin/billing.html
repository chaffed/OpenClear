Billing

OpenClear works out what each customer owes for positive pay services each month, from your fee schedule and the customer's activity. It produces a statement per customer, a report, and the files your other systems need: an account analysis feed, a hard-charge posting file, and an ISO 20022 camt.086 billing statement. It needs the billing:manage permission to set up and billing:read to view; both are part of the Admin group.

Set up the fee schedule

  • Open Billing → Fee schedule. Add common positive pay fees gives you a starting list priced at zero.
  • Each fee charges for one thing OpenClear counts. Monthly charges are per customer, account, ACH account, user, ACH rule or customer fraud model. Per-item charges include issued checks loaded, files received, checks presented, exceptions, returns (check and ACH), items paid, automatic decisions, stop payments, payee positive pay images, ACH debits evaluated, unauthorized-debit statements, data exports, and SMS and email messages.
  • Enter your own AFP Service Code for each fee (and your internal bank service code if you use one). Banks code positive pay differently, so none is assumed. The codes appear on the analysis feed and the camt.086 statement.
  • Prices can have four decimal places. A fee can have volume tiers (each tier's units at that tier's price) and a monthly minimum.

Decide how each customer is billed

  • Account analysis: the charges go to your analysis system, which offsets them with earnings credit on the customer's balances. OpenClear doesn't calculate earnings credit; balances and the earnings credit rate stay in your core.
  • Hard charge: the total is debited from the customer's billing account.
  • Exempt: nothing is charged. The statement still shows what would have applied, as waived.
  • Set the default for customers in Billing → Settings, and each customer's own method, billing account, discount, negotiated prices and waivers from the customer's page (Billing terms).
  • Your bank's own accounts (not assigned to a customer) and bank-wide services such as SMS appear on a separate bank-wide statement, usually exempt.

Close a month

  1. On Billing, choose the month. The figures are live until you finalize.
  2. Check the statements, then Finalize. That saves a fixed copy that later price or terms changes can't alter.
  3. Download the files from the finalized month:
    • Account analysis feed (CSV): analysis customers' service lines at their billing accounts, for your analysis system.
    • Hard-charge postings (CSV): one debit per hard-charge customer, for your core's fee posting.
    • camt.086 (XML): every statement in the ISO 20022 Bank Services Billing format, with your AFP codes. Check it against what your analysis system or clients expect before relying on it.
  4. If something needs correcting, fix it and finalize again. That saves a replacement version (marked as a replacement in camt.086) and keeps the old one.

Everything is also available through the API under /api/v1/billing.
