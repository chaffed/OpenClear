Stop Payments

A stop payment flags a specific check so that if it's ever presented for payment, it's caught and reviewed rather than paid automatically — useful for lost, stolen, or disputed checks.

{{ docs.screenshot("stop_payments", "Stop payments list") }}

How to

  • Place a stop — open Stop Payments and choose "New." You can stop a specific check number and amount, or leave the amount open to stop the check number regardless of amount. An optional expiration date lets the stop lapse automatically.
  • Cancel a stop — once the concern is resolved, cancel the stop from its detail page so a legitimate presentment isn't blocked.

Your dashboard's "active stop payments" count reflects stops that haven't been cancelled or expired.
