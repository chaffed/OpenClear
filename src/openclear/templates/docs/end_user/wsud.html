Unauthorized Debit Statements

A Written Statement of Unauthorized Debit (WSUD) is a legal attestation that a returned ACH debit against your account was not authorized. If you have access to this feature, you can review your organization's returned, consumer-type ACH transactions and sign the statement electronically.

How to

  • Open Unauthorized Debit Statement to see any returned ACH debits eligible for a statement.
  • Review the transaction details, then check the consent and attestation boxes and type your name to sign. Your signature records your typed name along with the IP address and browser used at the time, for audit purposes.

This is a customer-side, opt-in feature — if you don't see it in your navigation, ask your administrator to grant the "Sign a Written Statement of Unauthorized Debit" permission to your security group.
