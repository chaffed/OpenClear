Unauthorized Debit Statements

If an ACH debit was taken from your account without your authorization, tell your bank at once. For a consumer debit (one coded PPD, WEB, TEL and similar), the Nacha Rules have your bank get a Written Statement of Unauthorized Debit from you before it returns the debit as unauthorized.

Your bank's own form is under Unauthorized debit statement in the navigation. It may be a page to read, or a PDF to download, print and sign. Complete it and return it the way the form says. If your bank hasn't put a form there, the page gives you its contact details.

You can still decide the item in OpenClear as usual: return it, with the reason your bank's list gives for an unauthorized debit.
