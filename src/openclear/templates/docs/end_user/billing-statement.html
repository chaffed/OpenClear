Your billing statement

If your bank has given you access, Billing Statement in the menu shows what you're charged for positive pay services each month.

  • Each line is one service: how many times it was used, the price, and the charge. If a service was used on more than one of your accounts, the counts per account are shown under it.
  • Account analysis: the charges appear on your account analysis statement, where earnings credit on your balances offsets them. Hard charge: the total is debited from your billing account.
  • A month marked Estimate isn't closed yet, so its figures can still change. Final is what you're billed.
  • Use PDF to download a copy for your records.
