Paid Items

A paid item is a check that has actually cleared and been presented for payment. Submitting paid items is what drives the matching process: each one is compared against recorded issued items, and any mismatch or unknown item automatically becomes an exception in the review queue.

{{ docs.screenshot("paid_items", "Paid items list") }}

How to

  • Enter a single paid item — open Paid Items and choose "New," providing the account, check number, and amount as presented.
  • Submit a batch — use bulk upload for a full day's or run's worth of cleared checks at once.
{{ docs.screenshot("paid_items_bulk", "Paid items bulk upload") }}

See Bulk File Uploads for the general upload and undo (back-out) workflow, and Exceptions & Decisioning for what happens next when a paid item doesn't match cleanly.
