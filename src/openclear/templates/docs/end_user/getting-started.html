Getting started

OpenClear is organized in three levels: your bank (the tenant) sits at the top, each of your bank's business clients is a customer, and each customer owns one or more accounts. Most of what you do day to day — issued items, paid items, exceptions — belongs to a specific account, which belongs to a specific customer.

Logging in

Sign in with your email, password, and (if your organization requires it) a passkey or one-time step-up. If your organization uses single sign-on, your login page may redirect you to your identity provider instead. After several failed attempts your account locks automatically — ask an administrator to unlock it from the Users screen.

Your organization's sign-in link fills in its short name for you; bookmark it. If you don't have it, choose Don't know it? We'll email you your sign-in links on the sign-in page: we email your address a link for each organization you belong to.

To change your password, go to Sign-in and security → Change password. If you've forgotten it, ask an administrator to reset it: you'll get an email with a link to choose a new one, which works once, for a day.

What you can see and do

Every action in OpenClear is gated by a security group — a named set of permissions your administrator assigns you. Two people signed into the same organization can see very different navigation menus depending on their group. If a page or button you expect to see is missing, it's almost always a permissions question for your administrator, not a bug.

The dashboard

{{ docs.screenshot("dashboard", "OpenClear dashboard with stat cards") }}

Your dashboard (Dashboard) shows quick stat cards for whatever you're permitted to see — open exceptions, outstanding issued items, active stop payments — plus a "getting started" checklist banner if your organization is still finishing initial setup.

Switching organizations

If you have access to more than one customer, or more than one bank tenant, use Switch organization in the left navigation to change which one you're currently working in.
