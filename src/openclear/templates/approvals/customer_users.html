When you ask to approve new customer users (Settings → New customer users, or a customer's own setting), someone a customer's side adds waits here: an invitation from its administrator, its own sign-on or its SCIM directory. They can't sign in until you approve them.

Rejecting removes their access to that customer; say why, for the customer's administrators, who are told either way. People you add yourself never wait.
