Incident and maintenance notices

Tell the banks an incident affects as soon as you've determined it: one that has disrupted or degraded their positive pay service, or is likely to, for four hours or more (12 CFR 304.24), or a cybersecurity event that affects a bank's systems or data (23 NYCRR 500.11). Announce scheduled maintenance beforehand. Each notice is emailed to each bank's incident contacts (or its CEO and CIO), and shown to its administrators until one acknowledges it. The runbook's Incidents section has the steps.
