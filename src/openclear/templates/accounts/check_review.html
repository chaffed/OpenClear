  • Positive pay (the default): presented checks are matched against the customer's issued-check file, and only a mismatch reaches the customer.
  • Reverse positive pay: for a customer who doesn't send an issued-check file. Every check presented on the account is an item for the customer to review, marked To review. The customer returns the ones they don't recognise by the cut-off, and the rest are paid.

A check with a real problem (a stop, a duplicate, a void) is shown as that problem, and follows the customer's own default decision: it's never paid just because nobody returned it.

Turning reverse positive pay on pays checks nobody reviewed, so it waits for a second administrator when your bank asks for one. Agree it with the customer in writing first.
