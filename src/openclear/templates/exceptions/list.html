An exception is a presented check or ACH transaction that didn't match what was expected. Someone decides whether to pay or return it.

  • The queue opens on Needs attention: items that are still open, or whose recommendation is awaiting approval. The oldest are listed first. Use Status to see decided items too.
  • Why it's here says which checks failed, for example "Amount mismatch" or "Stop payment".
  • Fraud risk is the model's estimate of how likely an item is to need returning, where there's enough decision history to have trained one.
  • Decide by appears when a customer has a default disposition: if nobody decides in time, it's applied automatically.
