An issued item is a check you told the bank to expect — the "positive pay" file. When a check is presented for payment (see Paid Items), it's matched against these records; anything that doesn't match cleanly becomes an exception.

  • Outstanding — issued, not yet presented for payment.
  • Paid — a matching paid item cleared it.
  • Voided — cancelled before payment; a presentment against it will no longer match.
  • Stopped / Stale — an active stop payment, or past the tenant's stale-date threshold.
