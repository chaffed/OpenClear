A scanned image of a presented check, optionally linked to the paid item it corresponds to. Uploading one queues OCR in the background to extract the payee name and amount — if the extracted payee doesn't match what was expected, its linked paid item can retroactively flip to an exception even after it already matched.

"Bulk upload" loads a whole batch of checks (a ZIP manifest, or a native X9.37 cash letter) — each row/item creates both the paid item and its image together, unlike the single-image form which only ever attaches to a paid item that already exists.
