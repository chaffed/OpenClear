Each control is checked live, from what this installation is actually doing, each time this page opens. PASS means it holds, WARN that it needs attention, FAIL that it doesn't hold.

A control reports only what OpenClear can see. Disk and database encryption are your host's job: verify them with your provider and have your operator record how they're done.

The same results are at GET /api/v1/controls/status and in the evidence export.

Evidence export bundles, for a period, the system version, the rules and models in force with their digests, security groups, whether the action log verifies, segregation-of-duties breaches, changes and approvals, every decision with who made it, model-risk tests and these controls. It's signed with this installation's key; the JSON export carries the public key to check it with.
