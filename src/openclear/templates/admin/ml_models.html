ML scoring estimates how likely an exception is to be paid or returned. It's advisory: a person always makes the final call, unless a customer has chosen ML-determined default dispositions.

Your organization chooses its model in Settings → Fraud scoring model. The shared model learns from every participating bank and is run by the platform operator. A bank-only model learns only from your own decisions, and your administrators retrain it here. A retrained bank-only model replaces the active one only if it does at least as well on the same recent decisions and was trained on at least {{ min_bank_decisions }} of your own. You can always activate an older version by hand.
