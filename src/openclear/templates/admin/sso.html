Configure federated login (Okta, Azure AD, or any OIDC-compliant identity provider) for your own bank staff. Each customer can separately configure its own, independent connection from its own detail page. Every connection requires at least one group mapping — the federated org's current group membership always governs access, checked again on every login, not just the first — there's no "anyone who can sign in gets access" mode.

"Require SSO" hides the password form on this organization's login page — it can only be turned on once at least one connection has a group mapping configured, so you can never lock yourself out.
