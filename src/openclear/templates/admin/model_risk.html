Replay runs a past X9.37 cash letter or NACHA file through today's rules and compares each item with what happened to it then. Use it before and after changing a rule, to see what the change would have done.

Anomaly capture presents synthetic items with known fraud (a duplicate check, an altered amount, the wrong account, an ACH debit over its limit or from an unknown originator, malformed files) and checks every one is caught, and that clean items aren't.

Both are dry runs. The real rules run, with your settings and plugins, and then everything is rolled back: no items, exceptions, notifications or webhooks are kept, only the report. Each run is in the action log.
