Some changes need a second person: turning a protection off (dual control, segregation of duties, lockout, password and inactivity rules, required sign-on, security event forwarding) always, and sensitive settings and people's access when your bank asks. The change waits here until someone else who could have made it approves it. Whoever asked can withdraw it; a rejection needs a reason. A change expires after 7 days.

If nobody else can approve a change, it applies itself after 24 hours unless it's withdrawn or rejected, every administrator is told, and it's recorded as a critical security event. Every request and decision is in the action log.
