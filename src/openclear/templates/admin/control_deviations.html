A deviation is a stretch of time a check was at FAIL (or at WARN, for the checks you choose below), from the result that opened it to the one that closed it. Record what caused each one and what you did about it: that's what an examiner or auditor asks for.

The grace period is how long a check may stay at FAIL before the deviation counts against the control: the "Deviations dealt with" check fails while one is open longer than that.
