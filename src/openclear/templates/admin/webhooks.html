A webhook tells one of your systems that something happened (an exception was raised, a decision was made, an upload finished) the moment it does, so it doesn't have to keep asking. OpenClear sends a small signed message with the ids involved; your system then reads the details through the API with its own credentials.

Messages follow the Standard Webhooks format and are signed with the endpoint's secret, so your system can check they came from OpenClear. A message that isn't accepted is retried for about a day, and you can resend it from the endpoint's page.
