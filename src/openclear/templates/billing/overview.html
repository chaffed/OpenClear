Each customer's charges for the month are worked out from your fee schedule and their activity. Customers on account analysis have their charges sent to your analysis system, which offsets them with earnings credit on balances. Hard charge customers are debited directly. Live figures recalculate every time you look. Finalize the month once it's closed: that saves a fixed copy, which the analysis feed, the hard-charge postings and the camt.086 file are produced from. Finalizing again saves a replacement version, and the earlier one is kept.
